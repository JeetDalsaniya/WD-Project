Plot Focus:Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups (2026) is a dark action-crime thriller directed by Geetu Mohandas that follows the bloody and relentless rise of Raya (played by Yash), an ambitious orphan who rapidly builds a massive opium empire in mid-20th-century Goa as Portuguese colonial rule crumbles. Backed by his fiercely supportive older sister Ganga (Nayanthara), Raya successfully eliminates rival syndicates and expands his underworld grip all the way to Macau. However, his insatiable hunger for power is complicated by a tumultuous personal life—particularly his intense romance with a circus performer named Nadia (Kiara Advani)—and his ruthless choices eventually unleash a tragic cycle of trauma, betrayal, and a bitter generational conflict with his estranged, vengeful son.
