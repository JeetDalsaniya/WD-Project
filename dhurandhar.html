DHURANDHAR ~

Genre:Spy Action Thriller | Political Drama | Gangstar Underworld
Rating:⭐8.2
Language:Hindi | English
Director:Aditya Dhar
Writter:Aditya Dhar | Ojas Gautam | Shivkumar Panicker

Star Cast:Ranvir Singh | Akshaye Khan | Sanjay Dutt | Arjun Rampal | R.Madhavan

Plot Focus: the action-espionage thriller film Dhurandhar follows an undercover Indian intelligence agent named Hamza/Jaskirat (Ranveer Singh) who goes deep undercover in the dangerous underworld of Lyari, Karachi, to dismantle a cross-border terrorist network targeting India. By infiltrating local criminal syndicates, playing political power games, and earning the trust of powerful local figures like Rehman Dakait (Akshaye Khanna), the operative navigates a brutal, high-stakes world of crime and espionage to avenge past national tragedies and neutralize threats from the inside.
