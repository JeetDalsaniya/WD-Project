KALKI ~

Genre:Science Fiction | Adventure | Action
Rating:⭐7.0
Language:Hindi | English
Director: Nag Ashwin
Writter: Nag Ashwin

Star Cast:Prabhas | Amitabh Bachahan | Kamal Haasan | Deepika Padukone | Disha Patani

Plot Focus:Set in a dystopian future year 2898 AD, "Kalki 2898 AD" blends Hindu epic mythology with a post-apocalyptic sci-fi world. The story follows the immortal warrior Ashwathama, who wakes up to protect a pregnant lab subject named Sumathi carrying an unborn child destined to be Kalki, the final avatar of Lord Vishnu. They must survive against the ruthless totalitarian ruler Supreme Yaskin in the advanced city known as the Complex, while facing off against a selfish bounty hunter named Bhairava who is after the heavy bounty on the mother's head.
